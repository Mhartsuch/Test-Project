# Genealogy Research Report: The Ancestry of Avery Spellmann

**Research date:** June 10, 2026
**Goal:** Identify the parents of (1) John Spellmann Sr., (2) Doris Hoermann, and (3) Gary Smellie — the great-grandparents of Avery Spellmann.

**Method note:** This environment blocks direct page fetches, so all quotes were recovered via search-engine snippets of the cited pages (obituaries, grave indexes, family trees). Findings were cross-checked across five independent research passes. Before entering these into a formal tree, spot-check the linked obituaries directly — links are provided for every claim.

---

## The verified anchor

**John Winston Spellmann Sr.** — born **October 3, 1941, Fort Worth, TX**; died **February 25, 2020, San Marcos, TX**, age 78. Raised in **Seguin, TX**, where he met and married his high-school sweetheart **Doris Ann Hoermann** (~1959; "wife of over 60 years"). PhD in mathematics from Emory University; taught over 40 years at Texas A&M, Pan American University, and Southwest Texas State / Texas State University. Survivors included son **John W. Spellmann Jr. and wife Gerrie of Universal City** and grandchildren including **Avery Spellmann** — confirming this is the right family.
Source: [Pennington Funeral Home obituary](https://www.penningtonfuneralhome.com/obituary/john-spellmann)

## Lineage 1 — Parents of John Spellmann Sr. ✅ SOLVED

### Father: Homer Winston Spellmann (1913–1991)
- Born **November 21, 1913** in the **Rocky community, Gonzales County, TX**; died **October 1, 1991 in Seguin, TX**; buried October 3, 1991 at the family's own **Spellmann Cemetery, Nopal, DeWitt County, TX**.
- **CONFIRMED** as John's father by John Sr.'s own obituary: *"He was born to Homer and Velma Spellmann on October 3, 1941 in Ft. Worth, Texas."* Full identity from the Schliesser–Hicks Family Tree (which lists son John Winston Spellmann, b. Oct 3, 1941, Fort Worth — exact match, including the shared middle name "Winston") and an SSDI-type death record ("Homer W Spellmann of Seguin... born November 21, 1913, died... October 1, 1991").
- Sources: [Pennington obituary](https://www.penningtonfuneralhome.com/obituary/john-spellmann) · [Schliesser–Hicks tree](http://www.martha-jane-hicks.com/b261.html) · [Geneanet mirror](https://gw.geneanet.org/marthahicks?lang=en&m=N&v=SPELLMANN) · [AncientFaces death record](https://www.ancientfaces.com/surname/spellmann-family-history/192011)

### Mother: Velma Caroline Marie (Stanchos) Spellmann (1917–1996)
- Born **1917** (Gruenau/Yorktown area, DeWitt County); died **1996**; married Homer in **1938**.
- Maiden name **Stanchos** — from the Geneanet/Hicks tree, and **independently corroborated** by the obituary of her sister Lorene Hedwig (Stanchos) Koopmann (1924–2020, Yorktown), which lists *"sisters Velma Spellman and Marian Sheehan, brother Marvin Stanchos, and nephew John Spellman"* — tying Velma née Stanchos directly to John as her son.
- She appears in the Seguin Gazette-Enterprise of Dec 19, 1993 as "Mrs. Velma Spellmann" (alive in Seguin then).
- Sources: [Geneanet](https://gw.geneanet.org/marthahicks?lang=en&m=N&v=SPELLMANN) · [Lorene Koopmann obituary, Massey Funeral Home](https://www.masseyfh.com/obituary/Lorene-Koopmann) · [Newspapers.com page](https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/13727466/)

### Bonus — the next generations back

**Homer's parents (John Sr.'s paternal grandparents):**
- **Frank Thomas Spellmann (1879–1960)** m. 1911 **Mary McKinney (1893–1963)**, of the Rocky community, Gonzales County.
- Confirmed by the obituary of Homer's sister Marie Janice (Spellmann) Edwards (1924–2019): *"born July 27, 1924 to the late Frank Thomas and Mary (McKinney) Spellmann in the Rocky Community of Gonzales County... preceded in death by brothers Rudolph Spellmann, Homer Spellmann, Wilfred Spellmann, Arthur Spellmann, sister Annabel Spellmann Royal"* — interred at the same Spellmann Cemetery.
- Homer's siblings: **Rudolph, Wilfred, Arthur, Marie Janice (Edwards), Annabel (Royal)**.
- Source: [Marie Edwards obituary, Massey Funeral Home](https://www.masseyfh.com/obituary/Marie-Edwards)

**Velma's parents (John Sr.'s maternal grandparents):**
- **Ferdinand H. Stanchos** m. **Hedwig Sauermilch**, of the **Gruenau community near Yorktown, DeWitt County**.
- From Lorene Koopmann's obituary: *"Her parents were the late Hedwig Sauermilch and Ferdinand H. Stanchos."*
- Velma's siblings: Lorene Hedwig (m. Gilbert Koopmann), Marian (m. Sheehan), Marvin.
- Source: [Lorene Koopmann obituary](https://www.masseyfh.com/obituary/Lorene-Koopmann)

**Deep ancestry (tree-sourced, treat as probable/speculative until records are pulled):**
- The Spellmann line traces to German immigrant **Franz Heinrich (Spielmann) Spellmann** (b. 1822, Nordrhein-Westfalen, Germany; d. 1901, Nopal, DeWitt Co.), with intermediate ancestor Ernst Christian Spellmann (1855–1915, FindAGrave memorial 15355942) — the family surname was originally **Spielmann**. [WikiTree Spielmann-67](https://www.wikitree.com/wiki/Spielmann-67)
- The **Stanchos family were pioneer Lithuanian (Lietuvininkai) immigrants** — David and Dora (Scholze) Stanchos arrived ~1852 near Yorktown, among the earliest documented Lithuanian immigrants to America; Frederick Stanchos founded the Gruenau community in 1872. Founding members of St. Paul Lutheran Church, Yorktown; the family farm at Gruenau was a Texas Land Heritage (100+ year) property. [TrueLithuania: Texas](https://global.truelithuania.com/texas-1108/) · [TSHA: Gruenau, TX](https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/gruenau-tx)

So John Sr. was of German (Spellmann/Spielmann, McKinney by way of a Gonzales County family) and Lithuanian-German (Stanchos/Sauermilch) Texas pioneer stock.

## Lineage 2 — Parents of Doris (Hoermann) Spellmann ✅ SOLVED (one gap)

### Parents: Albert Hoermann and Lena Hoermann, farmers of Seguin, Guadalupe County
- **CONFIRMED** by chaining two of Doris's brothers' obituaries:
  - **Paul "Grady" Hoermann** (Sep 7, 1950 – Dec 18, 2024): *"raised on a farm in Seguin, Texas as the youngest of seven brothers and one sister... preceded in death by his parents, **Lena and Albert Hoermann**, his brothers and their spouses, Harvey and Leona Hoermann, Leroy and Wanda Hoermann, Bob and Dorothy Hoermann, Sidney and Kaye Hoermann, Jesse Hoermann, and brother-in-law **John Spellman**"*; survived by *"his sister **Doris Spellman**."*
  - **Robert W. "Bob" Hoermann** (Jul 29, 1933 – Feb 10, 2022): survived by *"three brothers, Leroy, Arlon, Paul 'Grady'; and a sister, **Doris Spellmann**."*
  - The predeceased "brother-in-law John Spellman" (John Sr. died Feb 2020) locks the identification to our Doris.
- Sources: [Grady Hoermann obituary (Legacy/San Antonio)](https://www.legacy.com/us/obituaries/sanantonio/name/paul-hoermann-obituary?id=57124948) · [Seguin Gazette copy](https://seguingazette.com/announcements/obituaries/paul-grady-hoermann/article_3fa52836-c6e1-11ef-97b0-e79420180e5d.html) · [Texas A&M Aggie Network tribute](https://www.aggienetwork.com/news/162536/roll-call-tribute/) · [Robert Hoermann obituary, Schaetter FH](https://www.schaetter.com/obituary/robert-hoermann)

### Father's full identity (probable): Albert Richard Christian Hoermann (1905–1999)
- WikiTree's Hoermann index has exactly one Texas Albert: *"born November 7, 1905, died February 15, 1999 in Seguin, Guadalupe, Texas."* Right name, right place, right generation (children b. ~1928–1950); no competing Albert Hoermann of Seguin found. **PROBABLE** — no accessible document joins the full three-part name to the children directly.
- Source: [WikiTree Hoermann genealogy](https://www.wikitree.com/genealogy/HOERMANN)

### The family
- **Eight children — seven sons and one daughter (Doris):** Harvey (m. Leona), Sidney "Sid" (m. Kaye), Jesse, Robert W. "Bob" (1933–2022, m. Dorothy Plasek; Texas A&M '55, 33 years Texas Agricultural Extension Service, County Extension Agent Emeritus), Leroy (m. Wanda), Arlon (m. Susan), and Paul "Grady" (1950–2024; Seguin HS All-American linebacker, recruited to Texas A&M by Gene Stallings in 1969).
- Grady "often touted that he was the **seventh son of the seventh son**" — meaning **Albert was himself a seventh son**, implying a large prior Hoermann generation around Seguin (consistent with Hoermann mentions in the German-language *Seguiner Zeitung*, 1901–1929).
- **Doris appears to be still living** (listed as a survivor in Grady's obituary, services January 2025; no obituary found through June 2026).

### Open gap: Lena's maiden name
Not recoverable from any indexed source. She died before February 2022. Best routes: Albert & Lena's marriage record (Guadalupe County, ~1925–1932), their San Geronimo Cemetery or other Seguin-area graves on FindAGrave, the 1950 census household, Texas Birth Index entries for any of the eight children (would state mother's maiden name), or Albert's Feb 1999 / Lena's Seguin Gazette-Enterprise obituaries (GenealogyBank/Newspapers.com).

### Don't confuse with (other Seguin Hoermann families ruled out)
- Alvin "Daddy Louse" & Reatha "Todda" (Davenport) Hoermann — parents of La Nelle (Hoermann) Downs (1934–2024). [Source](https://www.treshewell.com/obituary/la-nelle-downs)
- Eddie & Ruby (Knetsch) Hoermann — parents of Charles Edward Hoermann (1939–2026); family of the Campbell-Hoermann Log Cabin donated to the Seguin Conservation Society.
- These may well be cousin households (Albert being a seventh son), but they are not Doris's parents.
- Possible early-Texas ancestor (unproven): Fritz H. Hoermann (1838–1904), Carmine, Fayette County, TX.

## Lineage 3 — Parents of Gary Smellie ❌ NOT YET SOLVED (well-developed leads)

### What was established about Gary
- **Gary E. Smellie** (people-search aliases: "Gary Ehrich Smellie," "Gary Wanda Smellie"), born **~1935–36**, longtime address on Pruitt St., Longview, TX 75605. **He appears to be still living** (~age 90): no obituary exists anywhere for Gary or Wanda, and he's still in current-records databases. ([Spokeo](https://www.spokeo.com/Smellie-Smellie))
- **Spelling verified as S-M-E-L-L-I-E** by the Longview News-Journal of Aug 28, 1994 (wedding announcement): *"...parents Mr. and Mrs. Gary E. Smellie of Longview... Gerrie Lynne Smellie Spellmann, matron of honor."* The large Longview-area **Smelley** clan (Smelley Rd, etc.) is a different, unrelated family. ([Newspapers.com page 219443422](https://www.newspapers.com/newspage/219443422/))
- Children: **Micheal E. Smellie** (b. ~1963–64; later Grand Prairie/Houston), **Carolyn** (married James Craig Pee of Gladewater, July 30, 1994, First Baptist; settled Union Grove), and **Gerrie Lynne** (b. 1968, Longview; Longview High; married John W. Spellmann Jr. 1994). ([78209 Magazine profile](https://78209magazine.com/gerrie-spellmann/))
- "Wanda" may be a nickname: Spokeo lists a **"Wonderene Smellie"** as Gary's family member / Gerrie's relative — plausibly Wanda's formal first name (unverified).

### The structural finding
**There are no Smellie graves, obituaries, or vital records of the parent generation anywhere in Texas** — FindAGrave, BillionGraves, Legacy.com, TXGenWeb/Gregg County cemetery indexes, and funeral-home archives all come up empty. Gary (b. ~1935) most likely moved to Longview as a young adult (family documented there from ~1963), and **his parents lived and died outside Texas.**

### Leads (in order of promise)
1. **"Dee Smellie" and "Edith Smellie"** appear in son Michael Smellie's people-search relative graph (alongside Gary, Katie, Wanda) — generationally plausible as Gary's parents who survived into the 1990s+ database era. SPECULATIVE. ([UnMask](https://unmask.com/Michael-Smellie/))
2. **Northwest Ohio connection:** a death notice for **"Rev. Larry Smellie"** ran in the Defiance, Ohio *Crescent-News* on Oct 11, 2014; "Larry Gene Smellie" and "Mary Elisabeth Smellie" appear in related people-search graphs; and Michael Smellie's old email was at bright.net, a northwest-Ohio ISP. If Rev. Larry was Gary's brother, his obituary would likely name their parents. SPECULATIVE.
3. Gary's middle name **"Ehrich"** (a German surname) could be his mother's maiden name — a common naming custom. SPECULATIVE.

### Ruled out
- Robert Gavin Smellie (1919, Canton OH – 2013, San Antonio; parents Donald & Margaret; sons Roland & Mark — no Gary, no Longview). ([Porter Loring](https://www.porterloring.com/obituaries/Robert-Gavin-Smellie?obId=18165745))
- The Utah/Idaho Smellie–Smaellie clan; the Toronto polo-club Gary Smellie; Uwarda Smellie of Longview *Washington*.

### The practical shortcut
Since Gary appears to be living, **the family can simply ask him** — one conversation would beat weeks of archive work. Verify alive/deceased status with the family first; if he has passed recently, his obituary (Rader or Welch Funeral Home, Longview) will name his parents and birthplace.
