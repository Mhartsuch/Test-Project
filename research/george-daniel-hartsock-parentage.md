# The Parentage of George Daniel Hartsock (b. ~1790–1800)

**Research report — June 12, 2026**

## The question

Identify the parents of **George Daniel Hartsock**, born roughly 1790–1800, presumed USA.
Surname variants considered: Hartsock, Hartsuch, Hartsough, Hartsoch, Hartsook, Hartsoe,
Hartsuck, Hartzog, Hertzog, Herzog. The requesting family's present-day spelling is
**Hartsuch**, which constrains which descendant line is most likely relevant.

## Methodology and an important evidence caveat

Research was conducted as a fan-out of five parallel web-search investigations
(candidate enumeration; Hartsuch-spelling trace-back; early family-cluster mapping;
primary-record hunt; graves/obituaries/compiled genealogies), followed by a three-voter
adversarial verification pass on every load-bearing claim and a targeted hunt for the
modern Hartsuch line's earliest documented ancestor.

> **Caveat:** this research environment's network policy blocked direct page retrieval
> (HTTP 403) from every genealogy site tested (FamilySearch, WikiTree, Findagrave, Geni,
> MyHeritage, Ancestry, Genealogy.com, RootsWeb, archive.org). **All evidence below was
> obtained from search-engine result snippets**, cross-checked across independently
> phrased queries, but not read from the full source pages. Every citation should be
> opened and confirmed by a human before being entered into a family tree. Paywalled
> record sets (Ancestry, Fold3, full FamilySearch records) were out of reach entirely.

## 1. No indexed person named "George Daniel Hartsock" (b. 1785–1805) was found

Across ~130 searches by five independent agents, **no database, tree, grave index, or
record index reachable from this environment contains a person named exactly "George
Daniel Hartsock"** (or "George D." in any variant spelling) born 1785–1805. Findagrave
likewise surfaced no George Hartsock/Hartsough/Hartsook/Hartsoe/Hartsuch memorial born
in that window. This strongly suggests the name as remembered is either (a) a
conflation of two related men (a George and a Daniel), (b) a man indexed under only one
of his given names, or (c) a man absent from indexed online sources — all three
possibilities are developed below.

Candidates 2 and 3 belong to **Hartzog** families (South Carolina and Berks Co.

Candidate 1 is the best-documented George near the window, though his birth (~1781/82)
falls just before it.

## 3. The family framework: one trunk, many spellings

All Hartsock/Hartsough/Hartsook/Hartsoe lines investigated (except the separate Hartzog
families and the separate Conrad Hartsock line of Centre Co., PA) trace to **Hans Peter
Herzog (c. 1700–1763)** of Raritan, Somerset Co., New Jersey, who moved ~1750s to
Frederick Co., Maryland, with wife **Anna Margaretha Kuntze (1712–1784)**. Children
baptized at the First Reformed Church, Raritan, NJ, include Peter II (b. 1733), Nicholas
(chr. 1735), **Hans George (chr. 1737/38)**, Henry (b. 1741), John, Engelbert (b. ~1750),
and Jonathan (b. 1753, per compiled genealogy at Woodsboro, MD).

Notably, the **1775 Frederick Co. MD Committee of Observation list and 1777 Oaths of
Fidelity spell the family "HARTSUCK"** ("George Hartsuck, John Hartsuck, William
Hartsuck, Henry Heartsock, Nicholas Hortsook") — the closest historical form to the
modern **Hartsuch** spelling, and evidence that the -uch/-uck rendering is an original
Maryland variant of this exact family, not a later invention.

Principal clusters mapped (patriarch → places):

- **Guilford Twp., Franklin Co., PA** — George Hartsough Sr. (1737/38–1788/89); will
  names sons George and Henry. The only *primary-record* "son George" found.
- **Woodcock Valley, Huntingdon Co., PA** — Peter Hartsock II (1733–c.1800), builder of
  Fort Hartsock (1778); sons Daniel (~1759), Samuel, Jonathan, Peter Jr., Isaac, John —
  **no George documented**.
- **Huntingdon Co., PA → Bloom Twp., Fairfield Co., OH** — Jonathan Hartsock (1753–1817)
  m. Hannah Guizebard; reportedly **14 children of whom only ~4 are named in open
  sources** (Benjamin, Jonathan Jr. b. ~1790, Isaac b. 1798, **Daniel b. 1796–1800**).
- **Fairfield Co., OH (Brethren/"Dunkard")** — Daniel Hartsough (~1759–1834), son of
  Peter II; sons unenumerated in open sources.
- **Frederick/Washington Co., MD** — Nicholas Hartsock (1735–1821) m. Catherine
  Elizabeth; sons William T. (~1757), **John (~1760)** — whose son was the George of
  candidate #1 —, Nicholas Jr., Peter. Also Henry Hartsock (1741–1812), whose family
  Bible was held in 1938 by Edwin E. Stoner of Westminster, MD, children unenumerated.
- **Centre Co., PA** — Conrad Hartsock (separate Palatine immigrant, arr. 1750);
  children Catherine, Henry, Christian, Abraham, Daniel — **no George**.
- **Chatham Co., NC (Hartsoe)** — Friedrich Herzog/Hartsoe (~1695–1780), separate
  immigrant; no George in the window verified.
- **Virginia → Tennessee (Hartsook)** — Engelbert Hartsook (~1750–c.1795); sons
  unlisted in open sources; line continued to Washington Co. VA → Overton Co. TN.
- **East Tennessee (Knox/Sevier/Blount) Hartsocks** — could not be verified from
  open-web sources this session; needs subscription-database work.

## 4. The Hartsuch spelling: what is actually documented

- **Earliest exact-spelling record found: William Hartsuch m. Elizabeth Barnett,
  22 Jan 1797, Baltimore, MD** (WikiTree Barnett-4936; date rendered 22 Apr 1797 in one
  snippet — unresolved). Children undocumented in reachable sources. *A son George born
  1798–1805 to this couple would fit the research target exactly — this is the single
  highest-value lead to chase in subscription records.*
- **The modern Hartsuch family** (the requester's probable line) traces backward in
  open sources: David Hartsuch (b. 1961, Iowa state senator) ← Grover Paul Hartsuch
  (1926, Pontiac, Oakland Co., MI – 2022, Davenport, IA) ← Paul Jackson Hartsuch
  (1902, Kendallville, Noble Co., IN – 1982, bur. Kalamazoo Co., MI) ← **George Wesley
  Hartsuch** (b. ~1855–1880, m. Agnes Isabella Ritter) — **where the open-web trail
  currently dead-ends**.
- Geographic pattern of the name: Baltimore MD (1797) → northeast Indiana / southern
  Michigan (by ~1900) → Chicago-area Illinois and Davenport/Bettendorf, Iowa (20th c.).
  This matches the Hartsough concentration corridor (Indiana ~15% and Ohio ~11% of US
  Hartsoughs) and the Hartsough pioneer cluster of **Oakland Co., Michigan**
  (Christopher Hartsough, settled Avon Twp. 1817, b. ~1778 Rahway, NJ) — the same
  county where Grover Hartsuch was born in 1926.
- Distinct-surname warning: **Hartsuff** (Gen. George Lucas Hartsuff, 1830–1874) is an
  unrelated family frequently confused with Hartsuch in indexes.

## 7. Recommended next research steps (records this environment could not reach)

1. **Open the two key compiled genealogies** — both unreachable here and the likeliest
   places a George b. 1790s is already documented:
   - John M. Hartsock & Leroy E. Hartsock, *The Families Herzog, Hertzog, Hartog,
     Hartough, Hartsock, Hartsough, Hartsook etc.* (FamilySearch Library item 367099,
     v.1–2; free FamilySearch login) — read the **John-son-of-Nicholas** chapter (for
     candidate #1) and the **Jonathan (1753–1817)** chapter.
   - Mary Mae Hartsough, *A History of the Hartough–Hartsough–Hartsock Family* (Allen
     County Public Library, Fort Wayne; St. Louis Public Library) — the source naming
     Daniel (b. 1796–1800) as Jonathan's son; check the full 14-child list for a George.
2. **Find George Wesley Hartsuch in the 1880/1900 censuses** (Noble Co., IN area) — his
   birthplace, age, and parents' birthplaces; then his marriage record to Agnes
   Isabella Ritter and his death record/obituary (Noble Co. obituary indexes:
   gen.nobleco.lib.in.us and noblegenealogy.org). This is the requester's actual
   missing link, two generations below the research target.
3. **Run Findagrave's own search** (blocked here) for George Hartsock/Hartsough/
   Hartsook/Hartsuch, birth 1785–1805, all states; also browse the curated "Hartsock
   Family" virtual cemetery (findagrave.com/virtual-cemetery/116647).
4. **Baltimore records for William Hartsuch**: the original 1797 marriage register
   image; 1800/1810 Baltimore censuses; Baltimore church baptisms 1797–1812 for
   children (especially a George).
5. **Maryland Register of Wills free estate search** (registers.maryland.gov) —
   Hartsock/Hartsuck estates, Frederick & Washington counties, 1790–1850; especially
   Henry Hartsock (d. ~1812) and John Hartsock (b. ~1760) — a will of John naming a son
   George would settle candidate #1's parentage.
6. **Fairfield Co., OH probate 1817 (Jonathan Hartsock) and 1834 (Daniel Hartsock/
   Hartsough)** — estate files should name all heirs, resolving both the 14-children
   gap and the two-Daniels conflation.
7. **The Henry Hartsock family Bible** (1938: Edwin E. Stoner, Westminster, MD) — trace
   via the Stoner family or Westminster historical society.
8. **Fold3 / NARA War of 1812 indexes** for George Hartsock (any spelling) — none
   surfaced on the free web, but a man born 1790–1796 was prime militia age.
9. **DAR GRS ancestor database** (form-driven, not crawlable) — query Jonathan
   Hartsock (1753–1817), Peter Hartsock II (Fort Hartsock), George Hartsough
   (1779 Cumberland Co. militia), Henry Hartsock (1741–1812).
10. **Y-DNA/autosomal**: a Hartsuch male tester against the Hartsough/Hartsock surname
    project would confirm or break the presumed Herzog descent independent of paper.
